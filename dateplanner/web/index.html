Seven questions

Let's plan something worth leaving the house for.

Tell me the constraints and I'll give you a route — where to go, when to be there, what it costs, and what to book ahead.

Where

Where are you two going to be?

A city works. A neighbourhood works better — I'll keep every stop within walking distance of it.

When

And when?

Leave the date blank for this Saturday. The start time matters more than you'd think — it decides whether you catch the sunset.

Budget

What's the damage?

For both of you, everything in — food, drinks, tickets, getting there. I'll aim to come in under it.

Last thing
